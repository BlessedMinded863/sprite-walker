Rig / Binding

Skeleton-first: preview Walk, Idle, Punch, Slash, or Kick on the bones before binding sprite art.

Facing-aware combat: Front/Rear limbs are detected from the current shoulder or hip positions, so traced left/right labels cannot silently reverse the attack.

Rig repair: if the art was outlined opposite the rig, use Swap L/R Rig Labels once instead of retracing.

Compatibility: V4–V10.2 rig JSON files remain loadable.

Interactive Pose Editor

Real-time posing: switch to Pose Edit, then drag the bones directly on Marius. The sprite updates in real time.

Best use: manually build punch, slash, kick, and special-move frames instead of relying on presets. If you use Auto Segment first, the body pieces stay more intact while you pose.
